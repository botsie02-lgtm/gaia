# GAIA — Project vision

**Purpose:** Create a world of evolving entities, initially observed through a top-down pixel visualization, where agents, materials, environmental processes and societies interact over time.

## Design principles

- The universe exists independently of its rendering and user interface.
- All agents — friendly, hostile, neutral, and future deity-like entities — should be grounded in shared simulation primitives rather than scripted as completely unrelated systems.
- Materials are finite and stateful. Crafting is ultimately a process driven by material properties, conditions, tools, and time, not arbitrary menu unlocks.
- Use *models of physical reality*, improving their fidelity where meaningful. Full atomic/molecular simulation is not a near-term goal or technically feasible at large scales.
- Agent behaviour evolves from inspectable mechanisms: observation, needs, memory, decision making and, later, learning.
- Preserve reproducibility via seeds, fixed ticks, state snapshots and testable rules.
- Growth in world size and agent count must be informed by profiling: chunking, simulation levels of detail, selective activity and persistence come later.
- Long-term concept: deity-like collective agents may emerge from accumulated societal records and histories, including extinction events. This is a research direction, not a current feature.

## What version 0.1 demonstrates

Small deterministic terrain, finite wood and stone deposits, independent settler agents, energy/rest, fetching/stockpiling, and one emergent hearth construction process with fuel/heat bookkeeping.

## What it does NOT yet demonstrate

Actual learning, consciousness, sophisticated path planning, material chemistry, energy/mass conservation, biological evolution, independent civilizations, deities, large-world chunking, or multiplayer.
